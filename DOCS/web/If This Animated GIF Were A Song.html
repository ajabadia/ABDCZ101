If This Animated GIF Were A Song

2020-04-07

I made the animated GIF below to accompany the article about the project “Casio CZ-1 Spelunking REMIXED”.

Animated GIF

As I looked at the animated GIF, I thought, “What if this animated GIF were a song?” It could flip through the sounds at the same speed. Then next thing you know, there is a song titled “If This Animated Gif Were A Song”. You can play it with the audio player below.

If This Animated GIF Were A Song

Now we have a song, built to match an animated GIF... If the two were in sync, wouldn’t we have a music video? Lo and behold, now it is a music video titled “If This Animated GIF Were A Song Wouldn’t We Have A Music Video”. You can watch the music video with the video player below.

If This Animated GIF Were A Song Wouldn't We Have A Music Video
